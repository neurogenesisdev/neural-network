# Increase Testosterone — reference

Merged from former `Areas/Health/How to Boost Testosterone.md` + `Areas/Health/Increase Testosterone.md`. Organized by protocol area. Where an item appeared in both sources, the better-formulated version was kept.

## Protocol: Strength & cardio

- Strength training *(both sources)*
- Cardiovascular training
- Engage in regular heavy compound lifts

## Protocol: Sleep & recovery

- Quality sleep + sleep hygiene:
  - Keep room dark and cool
  - Limit blue-light exposure before bedtime
  - Optional: 0.6 mg melatonin 30 minutes before bedtime
- Sleep naked (keep testes cool)
- Daily cold showers

## Protocol: Intermittent fasting / diet

- Intermittent fasting *(both sources)*
- Ketogenic diet (optional)
- Sufficient cholesterol intake: fish, eggs, bacon, cheese, etc.
- Plenty of cruciferous vegetables (kale, broccoli, cauliflower) — helps lower estrogen
- Keep body fat low (further estrogen reduction)

## Protocol: Stress / cortisol management

- Meditation *(both sources)*
- Power poses, massages
- Ashwagandha supplementation (see Supplements section)
- Vitamin C supplementation
- Fish oil supplementation

## Protocol: Social / behavioral

- Improve social status, achieve accomplishments
- Have sex regularly
- Stable economy (reduces chronic stress)
- Avoid solo ejaculation

## Protocol: Hydration

- Sufficient water intake

## Supplements

### Cortisol reducers

- Ashwagandha
- Vitamin C
- Fish oil

### Direct hormonal support

- L-Carnitine-L-Tartrate (increases androgen receptor expression)
- Creatine

### Essential vitamins & minerals

- Zinc (picolinate form)
- Vitamin D (5 000 – 10 000 IU)
- Magnesium (glycinate form)
- Iodine (supports androgen receptor expression)

## Avoidance

### Supplements / intake

- Excessive selenium
- Excess or too-little body fat (ideal range roughly 8–14%)

### Environmental endocrine disruptors

- BPA (plastics, can linings)
- Parabens
- Synthetic fragrances and dyes
