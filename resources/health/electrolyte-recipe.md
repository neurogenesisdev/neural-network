# Electrolyte Recipe

Salt-to-mineral conversion ratios for formulating a personal electrolyte drink, plus the LMNT reference formulation. Used by [[../../Areas/Health/daily-supplement-stack]].

## Conversion ratios

### Sodium (Na)

- NaCl:Na mass ratio = **0.39338**
- NaCl:Na conversion factor = **2.5420**
- **2.5420 g NaCl = 1 g Na**

### Magnesium (Mg)

- MgSO₄·7H₂O:Mg mass ratio = **0.098611**
- MgSO₄·7H₂O:Mg conversion factor = **10.14085**
- **608.451 mg MgSO₄·7H₂O = 60 mg Mg**

### Potassium (K)

- KCl:K mass ratio = **0.52445**
- KCl:K conversion factor = **1.90675**
- **381.35 mg KCl = 200 mg K**

## LMNT reference formulation

Per serving:

- 400 mg KCl (Potassium)
- 600 mg MgSO₄ (Magnesium)
- 2 500 mg NaCl (Sodium)
