## Neck

Four stretches targeting different angles. All start from the preparation position.

### Preparation

1. Sit up straight on a chair with legs spread wide and core engaged.
2. Relax the jaw.

### Stretch 1 — lateral

1. Lean forward and grab the right side of the chair with the shoulder protracted and core engaged.
2. Look forward.
3. Reach overhead with the left hand and grab the top middle of the right side of head.
4. Pull the head to the side, towards the left shoulder.

### Stretch 2 — diagonal downward

1. Lean forward and grab the right side of the chair with the shoulder protracted and core engaged.
2. Lean to the left and look past your armpit toward the floor.
3. Reach overhead with the left hand and grab the top right side of the back of the head.
4. Pull the head diagonally downwards, towards the left armpit / floor.

### Stretch 3 — diagonal upward

1. Lean forward and grab the right side of the chair with the shoulder protracted and core engaged.
2. Lean to the left and look upwards.
3. Reach overhead and grab the top back of the right side of the head.
4. Pull the head diagonally left down and back so as to look up and diagonally to the right towards the ceiling.

### Stretch 4 — jaw-assisted rotation

1. Lean forward and grab the right side of the chair with the shoulder protracted and core engaged.
2. Look to the right.
3. Place the thumb and index finger of the left hand on the left side of the lower jaw.
4. Push the head to the right.
