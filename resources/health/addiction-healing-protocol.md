# Addiction Healing

# Prerequisite

1. Acknowledge the full impact of the addiction and resolve to confront its power over your mind.

# Method

1. Re-Label
    1. Take on the role of the impartial spectator.
    2. Pay conscious attention to your thoughts and sensations.
    3. Remove the language of need.
    4. Recognize the sense of urgency as a manifestation of addiction, not reflecting any objective need to be acted upon.
    
2. Re-Attribute
    1. With compassionate curiosity towards yourself, ask yourself why these desires have taken hold of you.
    2. Place the blame solely on the brain.
    3. State clearly where the urge originated. 
        1. It stems from neurological circuits programmed into your brain in childhood. 
        2. It represents a dopamine / endorphin hunger in brain systems that early in life lacked the necessary conditions for their full development. 
        3. It represents emotional needs that went unsatisfied.
    4. Realize you are not responsible for the stressful circumstances that formed your brain and world view.
    5. With attention, consciously observe the addictive urge for the for the mere effect of an automatic brain mechanism that it is and let it go.
    6. Realize there are better sources of dopamine / endorphins and more satisfying ways of have your need for vitality and intimacy met.
    
3. Re-Focus
    1. Buy yourself time by engaging in other activities. 
        1. Do something enjoy (Optimally something beneficial, like physical activity).
    2. It is not important what you feel but how you act. Exercise free won't. 
    3. This is not a sprint, but a marathon. Success will come in small increments.
    
4. Re-Value (Devalue)
    1. Realize the real disastrous impact of the addiction in your life.
    2. Remind yourself of why you have gone through the trouble of applying these steps.
    3. Remember that your brain has assigned a falsely high value to the addictive object (Salience attribution).
        1. Addiction has taken over your attachment, reward, and incentive motivation circuits. 
        2. Brain circuits including the orbital frontal cortex falsely ascribe the addiction as a source of love, vitality, intimacy, creativity, and honest endeavor. 
    4. De-Value the false gold. Assign to it its proper worth. Less than nothing.
        1. Ask yourself what this addictive urge has done for you.
        2. What has been the interpersonal value of the urge in your relationships. 
        3. What happened at specific moments in the past when you allowed this urge to rule you? What will happen today?
        4. Do all this without judging yourself. 
    5. Anticipate the return of these urges.
    6. Accept that the addiction is there in spite of yourself not because of yourself. 
        
        
5. Re-Create
    1. Life until now has created you. It is out of ingrained automatic mechanisms that you have created the life you now have.
    2. Now it is time to create a life true to your values, passion, intention, talent, and capability and connect the love in your heart with the love in the world.
    3. By means of the previous four steps you are releasing patterns that have held you and you have held on to.
    4. Imagine the life you really want in place of a life blighted by your addictive need for acquisition, self-soothing, admiration, oblivion, meaningless activity.
    5. What do you chose to create? Consider the activities you can engage in to express the universal human need to be creative.
        1. Mindfully honoring our creativity helps us trancend the feeling of deficient emptiness that drives addiction.
        2. Not to express our creative needs is itself a source of stress.
    6. Express your vitality through the particular channels and at the particular speed nature forsaw for you.
    7. Write down your values and intentions with conscious awareness. Envision yourself living with integrity, creative and present, being able to look people in the eye with compassion for them and yourself.
