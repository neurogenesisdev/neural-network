# Cooking Notes

### Chicken, Rice & Broccoli

1. Weigh rice.
2. Wash rice.
3. Add water to rice.
4. Add salt to rice.
5. Start rice cooker and timer.
6. Weigh chicken.
7. Press chicken.
8. Season chicken.
9. Get out plate.
10. Lay out vegetables on plate.
11. Get out cutting board and chef's knife.
12. Cut vegetables.
13. Get out pots and pans.
14. Raise heat in the pan to max.
15. Add oil to pan.
16. Sear chicken.
17. Lower heat in the pan to mid - high.
18. Add vegetables to pan.
19. Clean cutting board.
20. Raise heat in the pot to max.
21. Add broccoli to pot.
22. Lower heat in the pot to mid - high.
23. Add salt and pepper to the pan.
24. Add salt to the pot.
25. Put rice on the plate.
26. Clean rice cooker.
27. Put Broccoli on the plate.
28. Clean pot.
29. Add chicken on the plate.
30. Add vegetables on the plate.
31. Clean pan.
32. Add greek joghurt on the rice.
