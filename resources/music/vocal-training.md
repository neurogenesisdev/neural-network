# Vocal Training

- Neck and shoulder stretches.
- Basic lip trill on a single note.
- Rolling lip trill (Up and down).
- Round mouthed 'U/Ooo' on 5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1.
- 'Ahh' on 1 - 2 - 3 - 2 - 1 (Smile in the eyes).
- Advanced lip trill on 1 - 5 - 1 - 8 - 1.
- Basic 'Vii' on 5 - 3 - 1 - 3 - 5 - 3 - 1.
- Any tone on a scalar run (e.g. C-major).
- 15 second 'ssssssssss'.
- Yawny 'Yahh' on 1 - 3 - 5 - 8 - 5 - 3 - 1.
- Cord compression 'Guh' on 1 - 3 - 2 - 4 - 3 - 5 - 4 - 2 - 1.
- Chest voice 'Hey There!' on 5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1.
- 'Do-Be-Do' on 1 - 3 - 5 - 3 - 1.
- 'No' on 5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1.
- Triumphant 'Yah' on 5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1.
