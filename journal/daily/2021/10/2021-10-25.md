## Reflection

"Become who you are" — Friedrich Nietzsche

"Learning is growing" — Unjaded Jade

"The obstacle is the way" — Ryan Holiday

---

Outcome independence applies not only to women but to life in general. Hope for the best, plan for the worst. Never get so swept up in one possible future outcome that you neglect other opportunities. Don't become so emotionally attached to something that you lose composure when it's taken away.

Gehe nie wieder zu einem Friseur der nicht fließend Deutsch oder Englisch spricht. Achte darauf, dass die Beleuchtung im Raum gleichmäßig ist. Nehme nie den letzten Termin bevor Feierabend.

Know what you want. Know what it takes to achieve it. Execute the plan without excuses. It's the hard moments that count the most.

Lass dir dieses verfickte Tattoo stechen. Du hast Geld und die nötige Perspektive um zu wissen was was du tust und dass es eine inspirierte begründete Entscheidung ist. Prove to yourself that you are capable of commitment.

Entitlement is a subconscious mechanism to keep you on the path of minimum effort. It actively prevents you from getting what you want by taking away the motivation caused by realizing the gap between where you are now and where you want to be. It's lazy, dishonest, and holds you back. Drop it.
