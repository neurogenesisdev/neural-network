## Reflection

Don't rest or preemptively celebrate or despair after having put a plan into motion and temporarily having no control over its trajectory. Trust the work you put in. Don't wallow in regret over having not done your best, but do learn from your mistakes. Always remember, what's done is done.

Don't hesitate at the precipice of starting something new and challenging or lose faith in your ability to persevere. Even the steepest of learning curves flattens out eventually. Stick to your chosen path.

Don't feel bad about having to (re-)learn things that you feel you should already know or be able to do because they come naturally or easier to others.
