# 📝 Journal
## General
I am feeling quite a bit of emotional turmoil recently. I met a woman through my job about a month ago and I think I'm a bit in love with her. One of the problems is that it's been about seven years since I've been in a romantic relationship, about five years since I've had fulfilling sexual relationship, and since then I've been keeping a lid on all of those untamed, neglected emotions, needs, and drives. Apart from that is that I can for probably the first time in my life see myself really deeply loving a woman, and the possibility of getting hurt, especially while I'm in a somewhat unstable financial, career, and living situation in my life, scares me immensely. I don't know how or if I should act on these emotions, if I should play it cool or confess my attraction to her, if I should give her space or show her how much I care by checking in on her more regularly. I have conflicting advice and belief systems floating in my head, stemming from pick-up artist and red pill culture to evolutionary and behavioral psychology, but also stoic philosophy and pop-culture romance. I usually think in systems, but it seems that if that is all I consider I'm bound to mess up this budding relationship as well. We've kissed on the second date and all communication and interactions leading up to it seemed to indicate that we were both crushing on eachother, especially the kiss at the end of the second date and the text communication that night afterwards were actually lovely. We exchanged various heart and kiss related emojis and reactions, and I made her promise to text me after ahe got back from her planned stay at friends in Mannheim for a few days to a week. Afterwards though, in the following days, I resisted the temptation to text her, in part due to not wanting to appear clingy, which she explicitly claimed to dislike in our second date, but based on her posts on social media, and the relatively dry response she gave me to the update on the website I'm building for her once she fills out the question and to-do forms I sent her (This is a different story, that needs some more getting into how we met.), I'm deeply regretting not assuring her more of how much I enjoyed the date and, asking her how her job interview went the next day, how she's feeling, actively planning our next date instead of waiting for her to text me when she got back to Frankfurt like a rule obsessed autistic person.
