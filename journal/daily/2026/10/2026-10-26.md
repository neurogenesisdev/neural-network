## Tasks

I think two things were major drivers of a sense of focus, well-being, and heightened productivity yesterday evening and this morning (noon to early afternoon):
1. Doing 10 minutes of alternate nostril breathing.
2. Doing over 150 reviews of my WaniKani Japanese Anki deck after literally neglecting it for months, if not years, at a time when it comes to consistent daily review. The 10 new cards have probably been outstanding for a year or more.
The focused effort and progress seemed to build momentum for the morning workout as well. Let's not forget the third item of yesterday, which is mouth taping. I exercise consistently and nostril-breathe almost exclusively during that, and only switch to mouth breathing for exhales on especially intensive training sessions. I have had a past operation for a deviated septum and, before that, received a delayed circadian rhythm disorder diagnosis, a sleep apnea diagnosis (for which I was prescribed a CPAP machine but refused to use in favor of correct oral posture in my day-to-day life), neck stretching, improving my posture and core strength, and mouth taping, which I've done sporadically. I find it does more than meets the eye. Today, I started off by standing in front of the open window, getting daylight immediately, and performing alternate nostril breathing and open-eyed meditation to make it bearable to stand for more than a minute or so.

I didn't wake up with my alarm, but I made my bed immediately after waking. I hydrated with creatine and electrolytes, and took my last water-soluble supplement of vitamin C (1,300 mg capsule). I went to the bathroom, brushed my teeth, washed my face, and applied my new skincare routine. Obviously, flask scraped its tarine as well.

Good morning. I made breakfast before exercise this time, and I'm considering doing this permanently. I made it protein-heavy. It's just scrambled eggs (6 eggs) and sausages. This immediately expands my stomach and makes me not dread a day of eating and having to stuff myself.

After that, I drank some coffee (3 cups), did my isometric training (30 seconds of each hold of Antrix bodyline drill), and did sets of 25 air squats, then banded rows, then crunches, then pushups, 4 sets each, totaling 100 reps for each exercise. Then I went for my 5K run, which I completed in around 32 minutes for an easy zone 2 session.

I didn't feel too heavy in the legs, and I've decided that this is probably the best order for me. I'm usually just too drained or ego-depleted to perform more than 2 sets (and lazy ones at that) of the strength circuit if I do the run in the second position after the isometrics.
