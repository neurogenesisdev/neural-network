I am writing this with speech-to-text through Wispr Flow. I've been avoiding making any journal entries due to resistance and avoidance of effort. Hopefully this alleviates some of the pressure and incentivizes me to at least get my thoughts out.

What went well? I stayed consistent with my set goal of doing the 10 km per day, 100 squats, 100 pushups, 100 situps, and the One Punch Man routine, albeit rather late, despite having a day off work because I missed the start of my shift and was replaced by another person. Just took the wind out of my sails. Not engaging in the normal morning routine of exercise, coffee, etc. Could have bounced back quicker, but at least I did some organizing of my password manager list, but not much else productive.

Besides that, did a little therapy session with Claude. I uninstalled Instagram and all the social media from my phone. Should probably do the same with chess. Perhaps the webtoons, manga, manhwa, whatever. The decision to use them deliberately in the browser on my PC instead of having 24/7 access on my phone was a major one, and I believe it is impactful for my attention span. Also, the decision to make meditation just a foundational habit, just as important as the One Punch Man routine.

I noticed that the increased exercise, the lack of drug use, even nicotine, which has been a staple for a long while in my life, has increased the force with which my mind pursues distracted paths, branching off with ever more speed and tenacity. Expected to be able to easily manage a 30-minute session, but I managed 15 minutes with struggle at the end. Definitely something I want to do in the morning when I have more energy.
