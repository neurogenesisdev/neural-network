1. 
I dreamt about being undateable, in a sense. It was a sense of getting older and losing opportunities romantically due to that. There was something about a business opportunity as well, and deciding which country to settle down in and place a factory in the pharmaceutical industry in a Scandinavian country. Northern countries and Russia were considered, France as well, but the competition there was high. Hungary was considered, but tourism there played some sort of role. There was this revelation that Japanese culture was the solution, or at the very least that they had figured it out: that there was someone for everyone there.

In the next scene, I was in Japan on this sort of roller coaster or Ferris wheel. There was a young woman who I thought to be slightly older, maybe late 20s or early 30s, but she was very attractive. I couldn't bring up the nerve to talk to her.

Behind me, there was some sort of guru that noticed it and made a point of going up to her, sitting down, and just asking her her name and her age. Weirdly, he just went back directly after and didn't continue the conversation.

I think I should make dream journaling a habit, as just the evening before I was questioning whether I dream at all in recent years. This was prompted by reading Andrew Huberman's new book, Protocols, and the section on sleep, specifically REM sleep. It's also just a good way to get awake and focused immediately after waking.

Aside from that, I think having some kind of romantic, sexual, or even involved platonic communications and relations with the opposite sex is conducive to my motivation, discipline, creativity, and inspiration for life, basically, but all my endeavors specifically. I made a download of a dating app for this, or I could just accept more of the promoter jobs that my friend Juliano has offered to me once before, which I enjoyed but haven't quite gotten used to the social role I play there and how much more involved it is compared to security work.

Next thought: I injected 100 mg of testosterone enanthate into the right ventrogluteal around 14:00 today, Sunday, the 8th of November. It's been almost 2 weeks, 13 days, since the last injection. I'm going to stick to a once-a-week schedule and only stay at 100 mg for the foreseeable future. It's much harder to get the dosing interval correct with a twice-a-week schedule, and the 200 mg per week dose was fucking up my skin somewhat major. 

I went for a ruck walk today with two 5 kg plates loaded into my roll-top, padded, and strapped backpack, along with three 1.5 L water bottles, two jackets from work, and my bag of plastic bags for padding. It was much more difficult than I expected, which in part could be due to the bridge holds and shoulder shifts that I did.

Before that, I need to pay better attention to my posture while sitting at the desk and eating, coding, simply consuming entertainment, and playing chess. These bridges are another candidate for daily work, probably added to the isometrics. The ruck is definitely what I default to if I do not feel like a complete run, but honestly, I might just make it the main driver of my cardiovascular work because the posture benefits are just incomparable to jogs or runs.

Aside from that, I still need to do interval threshold work by doing sprints followed by walks. 

Relating to the posture improvement at the desk, I definitely need to buy a laptop stand and get one of the keyboards from the storage facility, along with a few other items like a yoga mat. My guitar would be nice. The digital keyboard is unrealistic. I can't place that here. There's not enough space, but in terms of volume, it would be the better choice. You can play with headphones. You can't do that with the guitar. It's not half acoustic or half electric.

In the meantime, until I buy a laptop stand and get the external keyboard, I just placed my laptop on two boxes, which raises it slightly below chin level. That is enough to not inflame my neck, upper back, or lower back. I'm compensating now by installing a local speech-to-text model so that I can do most of my coding and exchange with Claude Code, or write in my journal or write blog posts through my voice instead of my hands. 

I had an exchange with Chantal yesterday by text. She sent me the message the day before yesterday. I took some time to respond. I was considering, first, if I may be punishing her for not contacting me sooner, but it was just the time that I needed. I hope she realized that as well, but I don't think it was meant to be.

I was, touch, kindness, and intimacy, and relationally to the opposite sex, starved for so long that I completely flipped from my initial aloof and self-reliant image to somewhat needy and anxious. As soon as the possibility of a relationship or something sexual was on the table, which wasn't too bad, I knew from the start that she was a bird that cannot be caged. If I had been more smooth or suave, we could have had sex at least once, perhaps, but it was always in the back of my mind that she wasn't ready to be tied down.

She's a fairy still, flying along and never landing with Peter Pan. I'm still grateful for the experience she gave me and for thawing my cold and withering heart. After years of neglect and self-denial, she inspired me to start my business and to engage in this fitness journey. Even if this wasn't meant to be, it's clear that I'm not ready. I need to get my financial situation in order. I need to get my living situation in order. I need to be happy with myself and where I'm at, which I'm not currently.

She's like the muse, the catalyst to get me started on that way, the glimpse of what my life could be. Thank you, Chantal.
