# BDNF

## Idea
Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) is a protein that supports neuron survival, growth, and synaptic plasticity. It's a key mechanism linking exercise, fasting, and cognitive enhancement.

## Upregulation Methods
- [[intermittent-fasting]]
- Aerobic exercise
- Meditation
- Certain nootropics (lion's mane)
