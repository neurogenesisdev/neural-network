# Intermittent Fasting

## Idea
An eating pattern cycling between periods of fasting and eating. Primary protocols: 16:8, 18:6, OMAD, 5:2. Benefits include autophagy, metabolic flexibility, and [[bdnf]] upregulation.

## Mechanisms
- Autophagy (cellular cleanup)
- [[bdnf]] increase
- Insulin sensitivity
- Ketone production
