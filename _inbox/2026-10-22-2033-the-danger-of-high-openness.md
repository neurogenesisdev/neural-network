# 2026-10-22-2033-the-danger-of-high-openness

## Capture
Being willing to consider diverse perspectives, assuming ignorance, remaining intellectually humble, and maintain boundless curiosity can be taken to far when it leads one to give too much benefit of the doubt to convincing characters with false or questionable beliefs, spread oneself to thin pursuing an excessive amount of ideas and projects, and get lost in the shadow of society and human psychology.
