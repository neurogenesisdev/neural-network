# Phone dictation shortcut

> [!NOTE] Summary
> A double press of the power button starts voice dictation straight into the journal section of today's daily note in Obsidian, appending after the last entry when it's the second or third session of the day. Optionally, the daily note is forced to the foreground at a set time of day. The point is to make journaling as frictionless as possible.
