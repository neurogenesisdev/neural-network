# ideas — index

Ideas to pick up when there is energy and time: things to build, learn, go to or join. One note per idea, from [[tpl-idea]]. The home dashboard lists them under Ideas, filtered by category and effort.

- `status`: idea, active, done or dropped. An idea that becomes real work gets a project card in `projects/` or a repo with a `STATUS.md`.
- `category`: build, learn, go or join.
- `effort`: hour, evening, weekend or ongoing.
- `projects`: links to existing projects or notes the idea belongs to.
