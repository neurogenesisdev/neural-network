## Change notes

### Primary traits (2021 → 2026-04)

- **Agreeableness**: 0 → 0 — stable at floor.
- **Conscientiousness**: 66 → 35 — notable drop (~30 percentile points). Worth flagging against life-circumstance context (hostel / depressive episode / jobless as of 2026-04).
- **Extraversion**: 50 → 34 — dropped ~16 points. Consistent with increased withdrawal signals.
- **Neuroticism**: 44 → 50 — slight rise. Mild-to-moderate depressive episode context.
- **Openness**: 95 → 99 — stable at ceiling; modest rise.

Extraversion + Conscientiousness varied most; Agreeableness + Openness stayed pinned at their respective extremes; Neuroticism drifted mildly upward.

### Diagnostic implications for typology (see [[enneagram-assessment]] Iteration 7 + [[socionics-typology]] Iteration 5)

- **State-vs-trait dissociation**: Conscientiousness and Extraversion primary-trait scores are state-depressed in 2026-04. True trait-level values are somewhere between 2021 and 2026 readings. The aspect-level SHAPE (Orderliness > Industriousness; Assertiveness > Enthusiasm) is the stable trait-level signature, unaffected by state.
- **Orderliness > Industriousness widened gap** (4→21 points) confirms **ILI Te-creative-under-load** pattern: Ti-demonstrative (Model A position 8) can build ordered systems; Te-creative (position 2) executes them less reliably under depressive-episode load.
- **Assertiveness > Enthusiasm persistent split** (56-pt in 2021; 41-pt in 2026) confirms **8-tritype-gut-fix**: the Assertiveness-elevation within an overall low-E pattern is the canonical 8-tritype aspect-signature.
- **Aesthetics tied with Intellect at 97** confirms **Ne-demonstrative active alongside Ni-lead**: Intellect (Ne-aspect) and Aesthetics (Ni-aspect) equally high means Ne is a genuinely-developed secondary channel, not merely a background function.
- **Aesthetics growth** (+10 over 5 years) confirms **5w4-wing development** — the aesthetic-sensitivity axis has deepened over time, consistent with psychedelic-experience integration + longer exposure to philosophical / artistic work.

### Re-test recommendation

Re-take the BFAS **after** life-logistics stabilize (housing secured, employment re-established) to get a non-state-depressed baseline for Conscientiousness and Extraversion. The aspect-level shape will not change; the primary-trait levels may return to something closer to 2021's values (C near 60, E near 50).
