### Resolution of Iteration 1-4 tension

- **Ni-dom vs Te-dom**: **Ni-dom (ILI)**. The Te-dom reading in Iteration 3 (LIE-Ni/ENTJ) was state-masking — captured an execution-forward period and mistook state for trait. The adult-masking-of-Fe-PoLR-with-recovery-cost (Q33) is diagnostic: "I have learned to mask and lean into things despite my temperament. Always going to be exhausted and crave solitude afterwards." Te-dom extroverts do not produce this masking-with-recovery pattern.
- **INTJ (iter 1) vs ILI (iters 2, 4) crosswalk**: Both labels describe the same type via different conventions. Standard **ILI ↔ INTJ** crosswalk holds; the rarer MBTI-J-P-by-extroverted-judging-direction convention would produce INTP, but that convention is non-standard.
- **Gamma vs Alpha quadra**: **Gamma definitively** (Q34 probe: "Dense intellectual conversation with one or two people you genuinely respect — ideas get sharpened, nothing performative, exchange has teeth" chosen over "Playful idea-generation with a group — cascade of possibilities"). Alpha explicitly rejected as draining.

### Key confirming evidence

- **Q6**: "I usually in a flash correlate the concept to other concepts and then understand what processes that involve these concepts interacting likely play out" = textbook Ni-dom process-forecasting.
- **Q22**: Grief → Stoicism → Solipsism → Nihilism → Escapism → Realism progression = Ni-lead meaning-metabolization. "It has shaped my world view. I carry it only in the sense that my personality couldn't have been formed without it."
- **Q34**: Gamma preference ("Ideas get sharpened, nothing performative, exchange has teeth"), with Alpha explicitly rejected ("B is entertaining for a while and gets draining quickly") and intimate-values-exposed flagged as leverage-risk (pure Gamma-Fi-distrust signature).
- **Q36**: Protection of loyal-person via "long-term, deceitful strategy... revenge" when direct confrontation isn't optimal = classic Ni-Te strategic-patience, not LIE direct-execution or LII diplomatic-distance.
- **Q33 + Q28**: Fe-PoLR with adult masking-and-recovery pattern — "against my temperament" self-report precludes Fe-suggestive-welcomed LII experience.
- **Biographical probe B3**: Rehab group-therapy-as-healing despite solitary-default — confirms ILI dual-pattern (Fe-suggestive-seeking from environment catalyzing internal Ni-integration). "I always have a meta-process happening in my mind regardless of being isolated or surrounded by people" = state-independent Ni-dom internal processing.
