## Tension summary

**Resolved as of Iteration 7 (2026-04-21):**

- Core type: **5** (iterations 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 — majority converge). Iteration 2's "7" was likely capturing a high-energy state; Iteration 5's "8" was capturing the 8-tritype-gut-fix as if it were core.
- Wing: **4** (iterations 1, 3, 4, 7). Iteration 5's "7 wing" was 8-tritype restlessness mis-identified.
- Instinct primary: **sp** (iteration 6, 7). The three sx-first stacks in iterations 1/3/4 (and variants) caught sx-visibility-as-channeled-into-non-relational but mislocated it as first-instinct.
- Tritype: **583** (iteration 7). Iteration 5's **853** was the closest prior attempt; it had the correct triads (Head-5, Heart-3, Gut-8) but misordered them with 8 as dominant.

## What would re-open Iteration 8

The typing would need revisiting if any of these surfaced consistently:

- Authenticity-drive consistently overriding achievement-drive on direct probes rerun 6+ months from now → shift tritype 583 → 584
- Sustained so-instinct activation in unstructured contexts (not just under therapeutic structure or incidental leadership) → shift stack to sp/so
- Se-tolerance / confrontation-seeking becoming actively draining rather than energizing → shift toward 5w6 (wing-change not expected; would require decade-scale change)
- Published-instrument results (iEQ9 or RHETI taken under controlled conditions) materially diverging — those instruments carry validated-sample-normative signal the meta-interview lacks
