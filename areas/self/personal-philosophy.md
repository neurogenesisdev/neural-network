# Personal Philosophy

> [!NOTE] Snapshot in time
> Personal code of conduct as originally authored (pre-2026; exact date unknown). Revise in place or append a current-state section when this is revisited.

In effort to adhere to a certain standard as a respectable, disciplined and well rounded human being, global citizen and a part of the community, I ascertain to...

- Not live for anyone but yourself. You were born alone and you will die alone. In the long term no one is worth your time, energy and well-being.
- Only act on a thought or feeling if it makes logical sense, you are aware of the consequences of your actions and it agrees with your sense of right and wrong and a logical amoral ethical system.
- Consistently adhere to a efficient and invigorating morning routine that assures the best possible start into your day.
- Always suspend all judgement of a concept, argument or person for long enough to assure a sufficient comprehension of the subject as a basis for confidently making an informed decision.
