# Positioning

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Who Luis serves, what they get, why him — in one line, one paragraph
> (DE + EN), and five brand words. Every bio, header, and design brief copies
> from here so the site and the socials say the same thing. Draft until Luis
> confirms the wording.

## Brand

- Name in public: **Luis Gordon**. Handle everywhere: **neurogenesisdev** (fallback: `neurogenesis_dev`; `neurogenesis` alone where free). Domain neurogenesis.dev (luis, 2026-09-15).
- What the name means (luis): growing, learning, improving, forming connections; a nod to neural nets — the technology he leverages.
- Brand words (draft, from the above): **clear · connected · growing · hands-on · calm**. Anti-words: agency, hype, "AI-powered", cheap.
- AI is the delivery advantage, never the headline. Sell outcomes: a site that brings inquiries, admin that runs itself, content published on schedule.
