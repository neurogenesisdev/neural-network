# Admin blockers go first, polish runs in parallel

> [!NOTE] Summary
> The §14 employer notification (due 2026-09-13) and the IHK booking were
> pushed behind "make the site and socials presentable first". Both are
> 5-minute sends with multi-week lead times; neither depends on how anything
> looks. Sent 2026-09-15 after the Mentor pushed back. Also: two accounts
> (X, Kit) had logins nobody could find.

## What happened

- Two zero-cost, long-lead admin steps waited a week behind cosmetic work.
- X account (created March 2026 for the SaaS) had no KeePass entry; recovery went to a Proton alias and hit rate limits.
- GitHub user renamed mid-session without checking dependents (remotes, Pages, Actions) — Architect has to clean up.

## Rule extracted

1. **Anything with a lead time (consent, appointments, registrations) is sent the day it is written.** Presentability never gates paperwork.
2. Every account gets a KeePassXC entry at creation: domain-named, login email noted, 2FA + recovery codes inside.
3. A handle/username rename on a platform other things depend on (GitHub, domain, email) is announced to the Architect *before* it happens, not after.
