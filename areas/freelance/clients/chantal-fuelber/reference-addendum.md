## Decisions (luis, 2026-09-25)

- **Areas:** yoga teaching, travel, paintings, cookbook. Where teaching and travel overlap (a teacher who travels), the site counts for both. Only sites from her fields; none from unrelated fields.
- **Curation:** Mentor searched 6–8 candidates per area. Luis took the Mentor's picks (4 per area). The client only ever sees the short list; the rest is optional browsing.
- **Answer format:** two required ratings per site, Style and Content (like / neutral / dislike). Optional ratings on six dimensions. A free-text comment only when she really likes or dislikes something.
- **Definitions are part of the form.** Each dimension's definition sits right where she rates, never just the name.
- **Delivery:** Google Forms, because progress autosaves across devices for signed-in users (drafts are kept 30 days after the last edit) and Gemini can generate the form. PDF dropped: not phone-friendly and needs a PDF editor.
- **Language:** English (her site will be mainly or entirely English; she lived in Australia and the US for 7–8 years).
- **Form text is generic and reusable.** Anything personal goes in Luis's own message with the link.
- **Freeze before sending.** Once the link is out, the form isn't edited: deleting a question deletes her answers. If a change is needed, she gets a new form.

## The six dimensions

| Group | Dimension | Definition shown to her |
|---|---|---|
| Style | Mood | Colours, photos and overall feel: the first impression |
| Style | Layout | Spacing, fonts and how much text is on screen: calm or cramped |
| Style | Ease | How easily you find your way around and find what you're looking for |
| Content | Offer | How quickly it's clear what the person does and how to book or buy |
| Content | Voice | How the site speaks: personal or formal, warm or cool |
| Content | Personality | How much of the person comes through: their story, photos of them, their life |

## Form intro (generic, reusable)

```
This form helps shape the design and content of your website. Instead of describing what you want from scratch, you react to real websites.

How it works
- Each section is one website. Open the link, look around for a minute or two, then come back to this tab.
- Give two main ratings: Style and Content. The next page explains what each rating means.
- Below them are a few optional ratings. Only fill those in if something stands out.
- Only write a comment if you really like or dislike something. A short note is enough.

Your progress saves automatically while you're logged into your Google account, for 30 days after your last change. You can stop anytime and continue later, on your phone or laptop. When you come back, the form opens at the start with your answers kept, so just click Next to get back to where you stopped.

At the end there's a list of more websites to browse if you're curious. No rating needed there.
```

Personal part (Luis, voice or handwritten message with the link): this replaces the hard open question; 30–40 minutes, can be split up; no rush, the text for the inventory can wait too.

Form built with an Apps Script (Gemini in Forms can't take a prompt this long or read .md files from Drive). The script creates the form, a "How to rate" page with the definitions (shown once, not repeated per site; grid rows show the short dimension names only), and the linked answers Sheet. Check after building: Publish, autosave not disabled, links clickable, phone → laptop resume test. Tested 2026-09-25 (luis): draft saves across devices; reopening starts at the first page with answers kept (hint added to the intro). Test draft cleared.
