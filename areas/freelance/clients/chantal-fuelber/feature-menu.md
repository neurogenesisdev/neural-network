# Feature menu & design directions — Chantal Fuelber

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Working note for the discovery package: every candidate feature sorted into
> v1 / phase 2 / later-paid, with the technical and legal side effect of each,
> plus four design directions to show her. Draft — Luis reviews, then the
> client-facing version goes into the discovery inventory.

## Sorting principle

The site is a hub for an existing 9.3k audience. V1 contains only what
converts that audience into assets Instagram can't hold, is buildable as a
static site in her own accounts, and makes her a *seller* of nothing. Anything
that involves payment, delivery, or ongoing publishing is phase 2 or paid.

## V1 — recommended (pick 6–8)

| # | Feature | Why it's v1 | Side effect to know |
|---|---------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Home with one primary call-to-action | The whole site serves one goal; the CTA is it | Needs her answer to "one goal in 12 months" |
| 2 | About / bio / credentials | Trust page; India training, ambassadorship | Certificate details must be accurate — ask, don't assume |
| 3 | Yoga offering + class overview + inquiry | Core service; where and how to practice with her | *Inquiry*, not booking-with-payment. Luis suspects yamayoga employs her and handles assignment/payment — if so, v1 links to yamayoga and "booking" is off the table entirely |
| 4 | Newsletter signup | The one asset Instagram can't take away | Provider account in her name (Kit / MailerLite free tier); double opt-in; Datenschutz section |
| 5 | Work with me / media kit | UGC and brand collaboration inquiries — likely her fastest revenue | Contact form → her email; confirm she wants this |
| 6 | Painting gallery + "inquire about this piece" | Makes the €2k sale repeatable without a shop. **Must-have (luis).** | Inquiry only in v1. Price-on-label or offers/bidding are her call; a real auction is a shop with extra rules — later, and via a platform, not custom |
| 7 | Social links + selected embeds (click-to-load) | Instagram video posts and YouTube add value on-site; nobody scrolls IG outside the app | Embed = consent. Click-to-load placeholder ("Inhalt laden") avoids the banner until the visitor clicks. Luis prefers embeds (2026-09-09); confirm with her |
| 7b | Support / tip link (Ko-fi, PayPal.me, Buy Me a Coffee) | Linktree-style "support me" for the audience | Link out only; the platform handles payment. Income is hers to declare. Question for her |
| 8 | Contact | — | Form or mailto; form needs spam protection |
| 9 | Impressum + Datenschutzerklärung | Mandatory | Impressum needs a *ladungsfähige Anschrift* (address where legal documents can be served) — a Postfach alone does not qualify. Options: her home, parents' address as c/o (they empty the mail), or a paid c/o service. She travels NY/Bali/Frankfurt, so decide early |

## Phase 2 — nice to have (free build, only if v1 is live and she asks)

| Feature | Trap |
|---------|------|
| Recipes / blog | Empty blog is worse than no blog. Only if she commits to one post per month; otherwise phase 2 stays phase 2 |
| Travel-vlog page | Needs video content that exists; embeds trigger consent |
| Bilingual DE/EN | Doubles content and review work; ask which audience actually reads which language |
| Class calendar with live schedule | Only if she runs her own classes and keeps it current; stale calendar = lost trust |

## Later — paid

| Feature | Why it's paid | What it changes |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Shop (prints, originals) | Payments, shipping, AGB, Widerrufsbelehrung, Pflichtangaben, Kleinunternehmer status | She becomes a commercial seller; different Impressum, tax picture |
| Online course / video sales | Payment + delivery platform, consumer law for digital goods | Platform account, recurring cost |
| Booking with payment | Booking tool + payment provider + cancellation terms | Recurring subscription; support burden |
| Automations (newsletter sequences, inquiry routing, content repurposing) | Ongoing value → retainer | Luis's first paid service line |
| Website care plan | Updates, backups, small changes | Monthly retainer; the only case where a maintenance obligation exists |

## Design directions (show 3–4, she picks 1)

| | Direction | Feel | Fits | Risk |
|---|-----------|------|------|------|
| A | Warm editorial | Cream/earth tones, serif headlines, large photography, lots of air | Yoga, food, travel — one calm voice | Reads as generic "wellness" if photos are weak |
| B | Bold creator | Strong type, color blocks, video-forward hero | The vlog ambition; matches IG energy | Needs lots of media now; heavier pages |
| C | Studio gallery | Paintings first, white space, art as the brand | Repeatable art sales; distinctive | Undersells yoga if that's the primary goal |
| D | Raw & organic | Textures, hand-drawn accents, her paintings as recurring motif across all sections | Unifies "raw", cooking, art, yoga under one identity | More design hours; hard to keep consistent |

**Settled 2026-09-09 (luis):** A with D accents — warm editorial layout and
typography, simple (she said she likes simplicity), with raw/organic textures
and her paintings as recurring motif. C rejected: too few sale-quality
paintings. B rejected: B means a media-heavy, high-energy layout — big video
hero, dense grid of posts, loud type; it suits a full-time vlogger with a
library of edited video, which she isn't yet. Her story-heavy posting style
feeds Instagram, not a website. Reference sites for A+D: the Architect sources
them once her "3 sites you like" answers are in.

## Beginner mistakes this menu is protecting against

- Building all nine v1 items plus a blog because she said yes to everything.
- Treating "booking" as one feature — inquiry vs. calendar vs. payment are three scope levels.
- Live Instagram/YouTube embeds without realising they force a consent banner.
- Bilingual by default.
- Anything that makes her a seller before she has decided to be one.
