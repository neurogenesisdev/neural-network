# Discovery inventory — template v3

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Standard discovery for every website client, as three Google Forms plus a
> shared folder: (1) a short **questions form** sent first, (2) a standalone
> **reference form** with curated sites, (3) a **materials form** sent after
> the call, plus a shared client folder for all later file exchange. English
> first, translated per client. Designed with Luis on 2026-09-25/26 from first
> principles (what does a one-page "about me" site need?) after Chantal and
> Luis both stalled on open design questions. Build scripts:
> [[discovery-form-scripts]].

## Rules

1. **No blank-page questions.** Anything that needs judgement is a choice list with "Other"; plain facts are short text.
2. **Easy out, no silent gaps.** Choice lists are required and always offer "Not sure yet". Fact fields are optional. Every nuanced question is followed by an optional field: "Details, or why you're not sure yet".
3. **Short first, heavy later.** The questions form is quick to submit; the reference form stays separate because of its length.
4. **The client only sees short lists.** Luis (or the Mentor) does the searching and pruning.
5. **Phone first, resumable.** Google Forms autosave works across devices for signed-in users (30 days after last edit). Every intro says the form reopens at the start with answers kept.
6. **Honest wording.** No claimed experience (see [[../standards/communication]]). English first; never assume German.
7. **Freeze before sending.** Deleting a question deletes answers. Change needed → new form.

## Sequence

1. **Questions form** → client submits.
2. **Luis curates references** from their purpose, features and style-scale answers.
3. **Reference form** → client takes their time.
4. **Call.** Agenda = every "Not sure yet", skipped or unclear answer + the questions of each ticked module.
5. **Written scope** → client confirms.
6. **Materials form** + shared client folder.
7. **Rough version** as the first build step, from both forms' answers. Included in the fixed price with one reaction round; every reaction becomes a written rule for the client's style guide.
8. Build → two feedback rounds → launch and handover.

Fallback: if the reference form stalls for ~2 weeks, a rough draft can go out earlier to restart things.

## 1 · Questions form ("Your website: the basics", ~10–15 min)

Order: why → who → what → how → when → formalities. Four pages.

| # | Question | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is your website for? | Importance grid, columns High / Medium / Low, as many per column as wanted, untouched rows = not relevant (Luis, 2026-09-26: a strict 1st/2nd/3rd ranking was too narrow). Short rows, examples in the description (long row labels get cut off): get booked or hired · sell my own products · show my work · professional home base · grow my audience · brand partnerships · earn from recommendations · be found on Google. Optional grid; an empty grid goes on the call agenda. + "what exactly should people book, buy, follow or contact you about?" |
| 2 | Who visits your website? | Tick all, required: clients/customers · students/participants · followers/fans · brands/partners · employers/recruiters · press · friends/family · not sure yet · Other. + "most important visitor in a sentence" |
| 3 | What do you already have? | Grid Yes/No/Not sure, required: domain · logo · brand colours/fonts · existing website · professional photos · written texts · social media accounts · a physical location (studio, shop, office). + links/details. A "Yes" becomes a materials-form upload; a physical location brings up map, opening hours, Google Business Profile |
| 4 | Which features? | Tick all, required, each with a one-line explanation: booking · online payment · shop · gallery/portfolio · blog/articles/recipes · newsletter · embedded videos · brand/partner inquiries · recommended products (affiliate/discount codes) · not sure yet · Other. + "seen something you'd want?" Each tick = a module (below) |
| 5 | How should visitors reach you? | Tick all, required: email · contact form · booking link · Instagram DM · WhatsApp · phone · not sure yet · Other. + email/handles/links to show |
| 6 | Language(s) | Tick all, required: English · German · not sure yet · Other |
| 7 | How should it feel? | Grid, rows Calm–Bold · Minimal–Rich · Professional–Personal · Light–Dark, columns 1–5, 3 = no preference, definitions in the description. Grid instead of linear scales because scales stack vertically on phones. Steers which references get curated |
| 8 | Web address | Up to 3 name ideas (text) + preferred ending (.com · .de · .net · .me · no preference · Other) |
| 9 | Deadline | Date picker (optional) + what the date is for. Tells Luis whether he has capacity and whether it's a rush job to price as one |
| 10 | Impressum | Address source (own · c/o friend or family · paid address service · not sure yet) · registered as self-employed (yes/no/not sure yet) · VAT ID (USt-IdNr.) (optional) |

Which legal pages a site needs (privacy policy, cookie consent, labelling of affiliate links, terms) is Luis's call from the modules, not a client question.

## 2 · Reference form

Per client, built with [[discovery-form-scripts]]:

- Areas = the client's own fields; only sites from those fields. 3–5 sites per area; overlapping areas count once.
- Mentor searches 6–8 candidates per area with a one-line reason; Luis prunes. Unused candidates go in an unrated "Explore more" section at the end.
- Per site: **Style** and **Content**, Like / Neutral / Dislike, required. Optional grid on six dimensions; comment only when strong.
- Definitions on a "How to rate" page, shown once, never repeated per site:

| Group | Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Style | Mood | Colours, photos and overall feel: the first impression |
| Style | Layout | Spacing, fonts and how much text is on screen: calm or cramped |
| Style | Ease | How easily you find your way around and find what you're looking for |
| Content | Offer | How quickly it's clear what the person does and how to book or buy |
| Content | Voice | How the site speaks: personal or formal, warm or cool |
| Content | Personality | How much of the person comes through: their story, photos of them, their life |

- Personal words go in Luis's own message with the link (voice or text), not in the form.
- First instance: [[../clients/chantal-fuelber/reference-addendum]].

## 3 · Materials form (after the call) + shared folder

Built by hand per client (file-upload questions can't be scripted). Uploads need the client signed in to Google; files land in Luis's Drive and count against his storage.

- Tick boxes, Done / Not yet: account email created (used for all site accounts) · card ready for the domain.
- Text: name as shown on the site · short bio · what you offer.
- Short answers: social and profile links to display.
- Uploads, one question per purpose, purpose in the title: 1 portrait for the About section · 2–3 wide photos for the top of the home page · one slot per ticked module (e.g. paintings: photo + title, size, year, sold/not for sale) · one per "Yes" in question 3 (logo, texts…).
- Settings: **Allow response editing** on, so materials can come in pieces.
- Shared client folder in Luis's Drive, `<Client> – Website`: `Materials` (form uploads) · `Reaction rounds` · `Handover`. At handover the client gets copies of what they need; Luis archives or deletes the rest (frees storage; deleting a form response does not delete its files).

## Modules (call agenda per ticked feature)

To be filled as modules come up in real projects. Each module adds 2–4 call questions, materials-form upload slots, and possibly legal items (e.g. embedded videos → cookie consent; affiliate links → advertising labels, privacy policy).

## Filing the answers

After both forms: read the linked Sheets, summarise into the client note with every fact marked `(client)`. Every "Not sure yet", skipped or unclear answer goes into a **Call agenda** section of the client note. Reference answers become likes/dislikes per dimension → rules for the client's style guide.
