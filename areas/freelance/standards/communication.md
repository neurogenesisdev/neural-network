# Communication

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Response times, meeting notes, written confirmation, feedback rhythm, language.

- Reply within 1 business day. If no answer is possible yet, send a status instead of silence.
- Send meeting notes to the client the same day as the call.
- Confirm every decision in writing, even after a phone call or a chat with a friend. A WhatsApp message counts.
- Send one status message per week during a build, even when there is nothing new to show.
- Collect feedback in rounds: ask for one consolidated list per round, not a drip of messages.
- Write in the client's language. Never assume German: ask, or check the language of their socials. When it is German, default to "Sie" unless the relationship is informal.
- Write client-facing templates and forms in English first; translate per client when needed (luis, 2026-09-25).
- Set a target launch date on every project, including pro bono, and put it in the first written summary.
- Never let a client wait without knowing when they will hear from you next.
- **Close every thread.** When you have what you needed, the exchange is not finished — send the two-line acknowledgement anyway (thanks, what you will do with it, when they will hear from you). Known failure mode (luis, 2026-09-16): once the information is in hand the reply feels pointless and gets dropped. It is the cheapest professional signal there is and its absence is what people remember.
- Two-minute rule: any reply that takes under two minutes is written now, not later. Anything longer goes on the list with a date.
- Claim only experience you have. No "erfahrungsgemäß", "in meiner Erfahrung", "typically projects fail because…" in client documents until the track record exists. State the reason plainly instead ("der Zeitplan hängt an den Inhalten").
- Read AI-drafted client text aloud once before sending; cut any sentence you would not say in person.
