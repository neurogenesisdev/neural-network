# Pricing

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Fixed price for defined work, retainers for ongoing, hourly as fallback;
> free work only against a written trade; client pays third parties directly.

- Fixed price for defined deliverables (websites, one-off automations). Retainer — a fixed monthly fee for ongoing work — for maintenance and automation care. Hourly only as a fallback for undefined work.
- Free work only with a written trade: testimonial + case-study rights + one referral. Still a written scope, still 2 revision rounds.
- The client pays all third-party costs (domain, hosting, subscriptions) directly with their own payment method, from day one. Never front costs. ([[../decisions/fdr-002-client-pays-third-party-costs-directly|FDR-002]])
- Quote a single fixed price per deliverable from the catalog ([[../services/_index|services]], market-anchored 2026-09-09). Ranges only in first conversations before scope is known.
- Every retainer is monthly with ≤ 1 month notice on both sides (employer consent is revocable).
- Register the Gewerbe before quoting the first price. See [[../business/_index|business]].
