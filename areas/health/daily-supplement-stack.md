Dropped: NSI-189, CDP-Choline, Alpha-GPC, Phosphatidylserine, NAC.
- **Iteration 2 → 3**: trimmed back to 3 time-blocks; dropped the Thorne multivitamin in favor of Life Extension Two per Day (PM Before Bed's position eliminated); dropped Aged Black Garlic, Curcumin, Sulforaphane, Acetyl-L-Carnitine (Iteration 2 "too many supplements"); added Uridine Monophosphate.
- **Stable across all three**: Magnesium Bisglycinate (dose varies), Omega-3 Fish Oil, Vitamin D3 at 10 000 IU, Zinc, Creatine + electrolytes (Iterations 2 & 3).
