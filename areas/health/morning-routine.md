## Iteration 3 — FINAL (current, 2025)

*Source: `FINAL MORNING ROUTINE – Rings + Band.md`. Self-labeled "2025 version – this is now permanent".*

### Daily Core Session (25–40 min, 6–7×/week)
Do 3–4 rounds of exercises 2–7 (45–60 s rest between moves, 2 min between rounds).
Exercise 1 (Wall Walks) is done once up front while completely fresh.

1. **Wall Walks**
   - Reps: 3–5 (5–10 s hold at top when possible)
   - Progression: Freestanding handstand hold → HSPU negatives
   - Muscles: Shoulders, core, inversion control
   - Reason & Order: Highest neural/skill demand → done first when CNS is freshest + instant cortisol/blood-flow spike

2. **Ring Pull-Ups** (main vertical pull)
   - Reps: Max strict → partials → dropset to scapular shrugs/active hang 20–30 s
   - Progression: Add weight later or one-arm progression
   - Muscles: Lats, biceps, upper back, scapular retractors
   - Reason & Order: Biggest back stimulus while grip is strongest

3. **Ring Inverted Rows** (main horizontal pull – formerly "Australian pull-ups")
   - Reps: 8–15 per set
   - Dropset: When you fail → immediately raise rings 10–15 cm and continue to failure
   - Progression: Lower rings → elevate feet on chair/box → weighted → front-lever progression
   - Muscles: Rhomboids, mid/upper traps, rear delts, mid-back thickness
   - Reason & Order: Directly after vertical pull-ups → pre-fatigued lats force more mid-back recruitment

4. **Ring Push-Ups** (main horizontal push)
   - Reps: Max controlled
   - Dropset: When you fail → immediately raise rings 10–20 cm and keep going
   - Progression: Lower rings → rings turned out → transition to ring dips
   - Muscles: Chest, front delts, triceps, serratus
   - Reason & Order: Antagonist to rows → perfect push–pull pairing

5. **Handstand Push-Ups** (wall-assisted or pike)
   - Reps: Max partials/full → dropset to partials or pike push-ups
   - Progression: Full ROM → deficit → freestanding
   - Muscles: Shoulders, triceps, upper chest
   - Reason & Order: Overhead pressing strength & skill carried over from earlier ring push-ups

6. **Banded Back Squats** (band anchored under bed post)
   - Reps: 10–15 + optional 1–2 pulses or 2 s pause at bottom
   - Progression: More bands → pistol squat progression
   - Muscles: Quads, glutes, some hamstrings
   - Reason & Order: Lower body power after upper body is done → legs recover fast, no grip interference

7. **Mountain Climbers**
   - Duration: 30–40 s all-out
   - Muscles: Core, hip flexors, shoulders
   - Reason & Order: Quiet, intense metabolic finisher → maximum heart-rate spike when fully warm

8. **Lying Straight-Leg Corkscrews** (lower-ab finisher)
   - Reps: 8–12 controlled per direction (butt & lower back fully off floor)
   - Progression: Hanging corkscrews → dragon flags
   - Muscles: Lower abs, obliques, transverse abdominis
   - Reason & Order: Core is pre-exhausted → forces true lower-ab recruitment

### "Abundant Energy" Add-Ons (only on complete/heavy days – pick any)
- Ring Dips → 3–5 sets of 1–5 reps + slow negatives (current progression goal)
- Ring Face Pulls → 3 × 15–20 (rear delts + rotator cuff)
- Banded or Single-Leg Calf Raises → 2–3 × 20–25
- Bodyweight or Banded Romanian Deadlifts → 3 × 12–15 (posterior chain)
- Ring Support Hold → Tuck / Advanced-Tuck / L-Sit → 3 × max time
- Superman / Arch Holds on floor → 3 × 30–60 s (erectors + posture)

That's it. Everything else is solved forever. Run this exact list and watch everything grow.

## Iteration 2 — Ultimate (rings-introduced, long-form)

*Source: `Ultimate Morning Bodyweight Routine.md`. Precursor to Iteration 3; same 8 exercises with expanded per-exercise prose.*

This routine is designed for a 25–40 minute morning session, emphasizing a cortisol spike, blood flow, moderate hypertrophy, cardiovascular conditioning, and skill training. Perform Wall Walks once at the beginning while completely fresh. Then complete the main circuit as 3–4 rounds with 45–60 seconds rest between exercises and 2 minutes between rounds. End with the core finisher. No separate warm-up needed beyond the first exercise.

The exercises are ordered logically: highest neural/skill demand first when CNS is freshest, then alternating push/pull for recovery and blood flow, lower body after upper to preserve grip, metabolic spike late, and direct lower-ab work last when the core is pre-fatigued.

1. **Wall Walks**
   - **Variations**: Wall-facing; nose-to-wall if advanced.
   - **Progressions**: Longer hold at top → freestanding handstand hold → HSPU negatives.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A (skill + isometric focus).
   - **Duration**: 5–10 s hold at top when possible.
   - **Reps**: 3–5.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Shoulders, core, scapular stabilizers, full core; builds inversion control and balance.
   - **Reasoning**: Highest neural and skill demand of the entire routine; done first while completely fresh for maximum CNS drive, instant cortisol/blood-flow spike to head, and shoulder pre-activation.

2. **Ring Pull-Ups** (main vertical pull)
   - **Variations**: Strict form, false grip optional.
   - **Progressions**: Add weight → one-arm pull-up progression.
   - **Dropsets**: At failure → partials → immediately drop to scapular shrugs/active hang 20–30 s.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: Max strict reps.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Lats, biceps, upper back, scapular retractors; primary back thickness driver.
   - **Reasoning**: Biggest vertical pulling stimulus; placed early while grip and pulling strength are strongest, sets the tone for back hypertrophy and scapular health.

3. **Ring Inverted Rows** (main horizontal pull)
   - **Variations**: Rings at appropriate height; feet flat or elevated.
   - **Progressions**: Lower rings → feet elevated on chair/box → add weight → front-lever progression.
   - **Dropsets**: At failure → immediately raise rings 10–15 cm and continue to failure.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: 8–15 per set.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Rhomboids, mid/upper traps, rear delts, mid-back thickness.
   - **Reasoning**: Directly after vertical pull-ups; pre-fatigued lats force greater mid-back and rhomboid recruitment for balanced back development and posture.

4. **Ring Push-Ups** (main horizontal push)
   - **Variations**: Rings turned out for more chest; depth controlled by ring height.
   - **Progressions**: Lower rings → rings turned out fully → transition to ring dips.
   - **Dropsets**: At failure → immediately raise rings 10–20 cm and continue to failure.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: Max controlled reps.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Chest, front delts, triceps, serratus anterior.
   - **Reasoning**: Perfect antagonist to horizontal pulls; push–pull super-set pairing maximizes recovery and blood flow while maintaining elevated heart rate.

5. **Handstand Push-Ups** (wall-assisted or pike)
   - **Variations**: Wall-supported or elevated pike; partial or full ROM.
   - **Progressions**: Full ROM → deficit HSPU → freestanding HSPU.
   - **Dropsets**: At failure → partials or regress to pike push-ups.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: Max full or partial reps.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Shoulders, triceps, upper chest; overhead pressing strength and skill.
   - **Reasoning**: Builds on shoulder activation from ring push-ups; placed as overhead push after horizontal push for complete shoulder development while neural pathways are still primed.

6. **Banded Back Squats** (band anchored under bed post or similar)
   - **Variations**: Single or multiple bands; optional 1–2 pulses or 2 s pause at bottom.
   - **Progressions**: Add more bands → pistol squat progression.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A.
   - **Duration**: Optional pause/pulses.
   - **Reps**: 10–15.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Quads, glutes, some hamstrings; lower-body power and size.
   - **Reasoning**: Primary lower-body stimulus; performed after upper body to preserve grip and allow shoulder recovery; legs recover quickly and don't interfere with pulling strength.

7. **Mountain Climbers**
   - **Variations**: Fast knee drives in plank; cross-body optional.
   - **Progressions**: Increase speed/intensity; add sliders.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A (timed).
   - **Duration**: 30–40 s all-out.
   - **Reps**: N/A.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Core, hip flexors, shoulders; full-body metabolic driver.
   - **Reasoning**: Quiet, intense indoor cardio finisher; placed late when fully warm to create maximum heart-rate spike and fat-oxidation effect.

8. **Lying Straight-Leg Corkscrews** (lower-ab finisher)
   - **Variations**: Butt & lower back fully off floor each rep; slow and controlled.
   - **Progressions**: Increase reps/control → hanging corkscrews → dragon flags.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: 8–12 controlled per direction.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Lower abs, obliques, transverse abdominis.
   - **Reasoning**: True lower-ab isolation; core is pre-exhausted from entire routine → forces maximum recruitment with minimal spinal flexion risk; perfect final movement.

### "Abundant Energy" Add-Ons (only on complete/heavy days – pick any)
- Ring Dips → 3–5 sets of 1–5 reps + slow negatives
- Ring Face Pulls → 3 × 15–20
- Banded or Single-Leg Calf Raises → 2–3 × 20–25
- Bodyweight or Banded Romanian Deadlifts → 3 × 12–15
- Ring Support Hold → Tuck / Advanced-Tuck / L-Sit → 3 × max time
- Superman / Arch Holds → 3 × 30–60 s

## Iteration 1 — Pre-rings bodyweight

*Source: `Bodyweight Workout Routine.md`. Earlier era — no rings, uses plain Pull-Ups; includes Glute Bridges + Banded Calf Raises that later iterations drop.*

This routine is designed for a 30-45 minute morning session, emphasizing a cortisol spike, blood flow, moderate hypertrophy, cardiovascular conditioning, and skill training. Start with a 5-10 minute warm-up (dynamic arm circles, leg swings, jumping jacks/high knees, and active hangs). Perform the main circuit as 3-4 rounds with 45-60 seconds rest between exercises and 2 minutes between rounds. End with the finisher and a 3-5 minute cool-down (light stretches for shoulders, back, legs, and core with deep breathing).

The exercises are ordered logically: skill/inversion work first for neural freshness, then alternating upper/lower and push/pull for recovery and blood flow, metabolic spikes later for cardio, and direct core at the end for targeted fatigue.

1. **Wall Walks**
   - **Variations**: Wall-facing or freestanding if advanced.
   - **Progressions**: Increase hold time at top; transition to full handstand walks.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A (isometric focus).
   - **Duration**: 5-10 seconds hold per rep.
   - **Reps**: 3-5.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Shoulders, core, scapular stabilizers; promotes balance and inversion control.
   - **Reasoning**: Included for skill training in inversions; placed first post-warm-up when CNS is freshest for optimal neural drive, cortisol spike via blood rush to head, and shoulder pre-activation without fatigue.

2. **Pull-Ups**
   - **Variations**: Wide-grip, neutral-grip if bars allow; focus on scapular retraction.
   - **Progressions**: Increase full reps; add negatives or assisted variations if needed.
   - **Dropsets**: Partials at failure, then scapular shrugs/active hang (20-30 seconds).
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: 5-10 full.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Lats, back, biceps, scapular retractors; builds upper-body pulling strength.
   - **Reasoning**: Essential for back hypertrophy and scapular health; ordered early after skill block while grip is strong, alternating with pushes for balanced blood flow and recovery, promoting moderate hypertrophy via volume and dropsets.

3. **Handstand Push-Ups**
   - **Variations**: Wall-supported, pike if freestanding; partial range initially.
   - **Progressions**: Build to full reps; increase depth or add deficit.
   - **Dropsets**: Partials at failure, or regress to pike push-ups.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: Max partials (aim 4-8).
   - **Muscles Worked**: Shoulders, triceps, upper chest; enhances overhead pressing skill.
   - **Reasoning**: Key for shoulder hypertrophy and inversion skill; placed post-pull-ups as antagonist pairing (push after pull) for efficient recovery, sustaining elevated heart rate for cardio while building on early neural freshness.

4. **Banded Back Squats**
   - **Variations**: Resistance band(s) around neck/shoulders, anchored on bed post; add pauses/pulses at bottom.
   - **Progressions**: Increase bands for resistance; move to pistol squats for unilateral skill.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A (focus on controlled reps).
   - **Duration**: 2-second pause optional.
   - **Reps**: 10-15.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Quads, glutes, hamstrings; promotes lower-body power and blood flow.
   - **Reasoning**: Core for leg hypertrophy and cardiovascular conditioning via large muscle engagement; ordered after upper-body to allow grip/shoulder recovery (legs recover faster), preventing pre-fatigue in scapular stabilizers from band tension.

5. **Glute Bridges**
   - **Variations**: Standard or single-leg; slow eccentric.
   - **Progressions**: Add hold at top; progress to marching bridges.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A.
   - **Duration**: 3-4 seconds eccentric per rep.
   - **Reps**: 12-20.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Glutes, hamstrings; balances quad dominance from squats.
   - **Reasoning**: Optional for posterior chain hypertrophy if squats insufficient; placed directly after squats for targeted follow-up while legs are warm, enhancing blood flow without systemic fatigue carryover to upper body.

6. **Banded Calf Raises**
   - **Variations**: On block/book/stair with band looped under feet; single-leg if advanced.
   - **Progressions**: Increase band tension; add pauses at top/bottom.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A.
   - **Duration**: Slow eccentric focus.
   - **Reps**: 15-25.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Calves; supports lower-leg strength and circulation.
   - **Reasoning**: Tests for calf development (genetic or not); included for complete lower-body coverage and blood flow; ordered post-squats/bridges as low-fatigue finisher for legs, quick to perform without impacting heart rate buildup.

7. **Mountain Climbers**
   - **Variations**: Standard plank knee drives; cross-body for more rotation.
   - **Progressions**: Increase speed; add sliders if available.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A (timed effort).
   - **Duration**: 30-40 seconds all-out.
   - **Reps**: N/A.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Core, hip flexors, shoulders; spikes heart rate for cardio.
   - **Reasoning**: Ideal for indoor cardiovascular conditioning without noise; placed late in circuit for metabolic peak when warm, maximizing fat oxidation and sustained HR elevation after strength work.

8. **Lying Straight-Leg Corkscrews**
   - **Variations**: Straight-leg with butt/lower back lift; progress to hanging.
   - **Progressions**: Increase rotation control; build to full hanging reps.
   - **Dropsets**: N/A.
   - **Duration**: N/A.
   - **Reps**: 8-12 per direction.
   - **Muscles Worked**: Lower abs, obliques; targets rotational core strength.
   - **Reasoning**: Direct fix for lower-ab weakness; included as finisher for hypertrophy via pre-fatigue from prior circuit, placed last to force better recruitment after core is smoked, enhancing skill in anti-rotation/control.
