## Squat

- Current Weight: 155 kg (5x5)
- Intermediate Goal Weight: 147 kg

1. Assume a stable, slightly wider than shoulder width stance while keeping your core engaged.
2. Grab the bar at double shoulder width.
3. Place the bar on your upper traps.
4. Tilt head down and to each side to move the bar to the most stable low bar position.
5. Step back and assume a stable, slightly wider than shoulder width stance while keeping your core engaged.
6. Lower down while keeping a straight back, chest out, and chin up.
7. Rise up explosively and lock out at the end.
