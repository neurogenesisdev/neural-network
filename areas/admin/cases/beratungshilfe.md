# Beratungshilfe + Schuldnerberatung

> [!NOTE] Summary
> Two free support channels that unblock several cases at once. Beratungshilfe
> (Beratungshilfeschein, 15 € fee) pays a lawyer for advice and out-of-court
> representation when income is at Existenzminimum — covers the landlady claim
> and the insolvency filing. Schuldnerberatung (free) negotiates Raten with the
> Jobcenter, Inkasso and the city.

## Facts
- Beratungshilfe: apply at the Rechtsantragstelle of the Amtsgericht of residence with proof of income, rent/housing cost, Bürgergeld or payslips; get the Schein, then pick any lawyer who accepts it (assumed — verify opening hours and documents on frankfurt.de / justiz.hessen.de)
- Living in a Notunterkunft and part-time income → likely eligible (assumed)
- Schuldnerberatung Frankfurt: several free providers; waiting times of weeks are common — book early (assumed)
- Mieterverein: usually a waiting period (~3 months) before covering an existing dispute; not the best fit for the landlady case (assumed)
